# Section 1: Under the `Discover` Tab (The Entry Salon)

### 1. What You See on the Screen
- **Top Hero Header:** Headline: *"Discover career paths aligned with who you actually are."*
- **Progress Status Bar:** Shows overall completion percentage (e.g. *58% Answered*).
- **Start / Resume Button:** Large emerald button to launch the questionnaire.
- **Three Core Pillars Row:**
  - *Adaptive Inquiry:* Dynamic branching without personality boxes.
  - *Authoritative Data:* Verified international job classifications.
  - *100% Private:* In-browser storage with zero tracking.
- **Nine Discovery Areas Grid:** 9 cards (Interests, Aptitudes, Skills, Work Style, Values, Lifestyle, Education, Constraints, Ambitions) with completion bars.

### 2. Exactly How to Use It in Front of Class
1. **Scroll slowly:** Show the clean hero header and point out the 3 core pillars.
2. **Point out the 9 Dimensions:** Explain that real career guidance cannot just look at your major; it must evaluate all 9 areas of life.
3. **Click the Emerald Button:** Click *"Resume Assessment"* (or *"Start Assessment"*). The screen smoothly transitions into the **Adaptive Assessment Console**.
4. **Demonstrate a Question Type:**
   - Show a *Priority Ranking* (click up/down arrows).
   - Or show a *Trade-Off* (Option A vs. Option B).
   - Or show an *Allocation Slider* (balancing 100% of weekly work).
5. **Click "Why We Ask":** Opens the drawer revealing the psychological reasoning.
6. **Point to the Uncertainty Checkbox:** *"I'm exploring this / not completely sure."*

> **What to Say to the Class While Pointing Here:**  
> *"Notice that Career Pilot doesn't start with a generic survey. It breaks human capability into nine grounded life dimensions—from what naturally fascinates you, to your financial runway, to how much autonomy you need. And during the assessment, you aren't forced into guessing. If you're unsure about something, Career Pilot treats uncertainty as a legitimate data point to explore later, rather than forcing a false assumption."*

> **The Wow-Factor Interaction:** Click the **"Why We Ask"** button on any question. The audience will see that every single question has a scientific rationale, immediately proving that this is a thoughtfully engineered system.

# Section 2: Under the `My Profile` Tab (The Exploded Self)

### 1. What You See on the Canvas
- **Central Profile Core:** Glowing center box with your name, "Profile Core", and verified signal count.
- **Six Floating Dimension Plates:**
  - *Interests & Curiosity*
  - *Aptitudes & Strengths*
  - *Skills Inventory*
  - *Work Preferences*
  - *Core Values*
  - *Growth Vectors*
- **Top Controls Ribbon:**
  - *Expansion Slider:* Slider labeled 20% to 100%.
  - *3D View / 2D View Button:* Isometric tilt toggle.
- **Right Inspector Dossier:** Appears when a layer is clicked. Shows signals, confidence rating, and evidence buttons.

### 2. Exactly How to Use It in Front of Class
1. **Let the visual sink in:** Pause for 2 seconds. The 3D layout looks striking on a projector screen.
2. **Drag the Expansion Slider:** Grab the slider and slowly move it from 40% to 90%. Watch all six layers expand outward in 3D space, then pull them back together.
3. **Toggle 3D View:** Click the *"3D View"* button to switch between orthographic 2D and isometric 3D tilt.
4. **Click the "Aptitudes" Plate:** Click the floating plate. It elevates forward with an emerald border.
5. **Look at the Right Dossier:** The right panel updates with your verified cognitive strengths.
6. **Click "Inspect Evidence":** Opens the audit modal showing the exact question prompt, your written answer, and the capture timestamp!

> **What to Say to the Class While Pointing Here:**  
> *"In engineering and architecture, an 'exploded view' separates the parts of a complex machine so you can understand how everything connects. We did the exact same thing for a human's career profile. In the center is your core baseline, surrounded by six floating intelligence dimensions. And this isn't just animation—every plate is interactive. When I click 'Aptitudes' and click 'Inspect Evidence', the system shows the exact question I answered and why this trait was inferred. There is zero AI hallucination and 100% auditability."*

> **The Wow-Factor Interaction:** Clicking **"Inspect Evidence"**. Professors love auditability and deterministic traceability. Showing that every conclusion traces back to an actual user answer earns huge academic credibility.

# Section 3: Under the `Career Atlas` Tab (Network Constellation)

### 1. What You See on the Screen
- **Top Network Constellation Map:**
  - *Column 0 (Left):* **YOU** (Profile Origin node).
  - *Column 1:* **Education** nodes (B.S. CS, Bio, Bootcamp).
  - *Column 2:* **Key Skills** nodes (Distributed Systems, Pipelines).
  - *Column 3:* **Bridge Roles** (Entry stepping stones).
  - *Column 4 (Right):* **Destination Careers**.
- **Recommendation Category Badges:** Direct Matches, Adjacent Matches, Interdisciplinary, and Unexpected Discovery.
- **Career Cards Grid & Inspection Drawer:** Cards with match percentage, salary range, and job family.

### 2. Exactly How to Use It in Front of Class
1. **Explain the 5-Column Network:** Point to the top graph. Explain: *"On the left is You. Then Education, Skills, Bridge Roles, and Destination Occupations."*
2. **Click an Occupation Card Below:** Scroll down slightly and click a career card (like *Distributed Systems Engineer* or *Bioinformatics Analyst*).
3. **WATCH THE NETWORK ILLUMINATE:** Point to the top graph. The exact lines connecting You → Education → Skills → Bridge Role → Target Career light up in bright emerald and cyan!
4. **Inspect the Right Drawer:** The occupational drawer opens on the right.
   - Show the **Salary percentiles** (10th percentile entry to 90th percentile veteran).
   - Toggle between **US ($)**, **EU (€)**, and **India (₹)** currency.
   - Point out the **Transferable Skills** list.

> **What to Say to the Class While Pointing Here:**  
> *"Notice that Career Pilot doesn't just recommend a job title; it maps the structural journey. Right here in the Career Universe Network, when I click on 'Bioinformatics Analyst', the graph dynamically illuminates the entire path. It shows the computational biology education required, the algorithmic pipeline skills, and the intermediate bridge role that gets you there. Every recommendation is grounded in international taxonomies like O\*NET and ESCO."*

> **The Wow-Factor Interaction:** Clicking on an occupation card and watching the network graph automatically highlight the multi-hop trajectory. This immediately proves that Career Pilot understands career progression as a series of stepping stones rather than a blind leap.

# Section 4: Under the `My Pathways` Tab (Route Generator)

### 1. What You See on the Screen
- **Target Career Dropdown:** Lets you choose which destination career to generate routes for.
- **Telemetry Bar:** Shows verified career nodes, graph edges, and validated routes.
- **Route Category Cards:** Direct Linear Pathway, Skill-Based Bridge Pathway, Alternative Education, and Interdisciplinary.
- **Sequential Milestone Cards:** Step-by-step cards showing phase duration (e.g. *6–12 months*), required skills, and potential transition risks.
- **Action Buttons:** *"Save Pathway"* and *"Compare Pathway"*.

### 2. Exactly How to Use It in Front of Class
1. **Change the Target Occupation:** Use the dropdown to select a career (e.g. *Cloud Security Architect*).
2. **Show the Route Options:** Point out that there is never just one way into a career. Show the *Direct Route* vs. the *Skill-Based Bridge Route*.
3. **Walk through Phase 1 and Phase 2:** Show the milestone steps: *"Phase 1: Build foundational key-value store prototype (3 months). Phase 2: Transition into Junior Systems Engineer (12 months)."*
4. **Click "Save Pathway":** Shows the badge update indicating it is saved to your personal collection.

# Section 5: Under the `Compare` Tab (Trade-Off Simulator)

### 1. What You See on the Screen
- **Side-by-Side Ledger:** 2 to 3 columns displaying selected target careers side by side.
- **Direct Attribute Comparison:** Salary trajectories, weekly working hours, autonomy scores, entry barrier, and remote work viability.
- **Priority Lens Simulator Bar:** Pill buttons for *Balanced Baseline*, *Max Compensation*, *Max Autonomy*, *Max Remote*, *Low Entry Barrier*, and *Values & Impact*.

### 2. Exactly How to Use It in Front of Class
1. **Point to the Side-by-Side Columns:** Show Career A vs. Career B.
2. **Click "Max Compensation" Lens:** Watch the comparison scores re-order with highest salary winning.
3. **Click "Max Autonomy" Lens:** Watch the rankings re-balance to favor strategic freedom.
4. **Explain the Trade-Off:** Show that the highest paying job often has higher operational friction, while the most autonomous job might have a longer runway requirement.

# Section 6: Under the `Roadmap` Tab (Execution Board)

### 1. What You See on the Screen
- **Action Velocity Meter:** Shows completed vs. total action items (e.g. *1 of 5 Completed - 20%*).
- **Three Time Horizon Buckets:**
  - *Next 7 Days:* Immediate small wins (reading a paper, auditing a repo).
  - *Next 30 Days:* Tangible projects (building an MVP, setting up tools).
  - *Quarter 1 (90 Days):* Substantial milestones (completing a verified artifact).
- **Interactive Checkboxes:** Square check buttons next to each task.
- **Print Plan Button:** Clean printer-formatted export.

### 2. Exactly How to Use It in Front of Class
1. **Scroll through the 7-day items:** Show that these are concrete, doable tasks rather than vague advice.
2. **Click a Checkbox:** Click a box on a task. Watch the checkmark appear and the *Action Velocity meter* fill up live on the screen!
3. **Point to the Print Button:** Mention that students can print their weekly sprint plan and tape it to their desk.
